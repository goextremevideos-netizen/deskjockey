Your first orders, from eligibility through your first reimbursed train.

  1. Meet the requirement. Have at least two weeks in county before applying through the Western Skies Government job application process.
  2. Complete onboarding. Once accepted, a Senior Engineer or higher can take you through onboarding.
  3. Bring operating funds. Arrive with approximately $15,000. You will be reimbursed for your first train.
  4. Stock up wisely. Stop by the Stoner Speakeasy at the Blackwater Saloon for lower-cost repair tool kits.
Information only — this board contains no application.

Three essentials keep the iron horse moving: water, repairs, and coal.

01

Water

Your top priority. Use water points throughout the map, position the coal cart beneath the fill, hop out, and pay $1 to refill.

02

Repairs

Inspect the front-left and front-right sides of the locomotive wheels. Carry repair tool kits before departure.

03

Coal

Collect free coal bags from the job menu at every station. Take at least six at a time.

Always choose the proper train when transporting passengers.

  • When a passenger call arrives, use /RESP. This shares your name and accepts the client’s request.
  • Actual players take priority over station missions. Real passengers are not charged, but may tip you.
  • Tell all passengers to use /scenario to sit down safely.
  • While seated on the train, use your R key bind for announcements. Use departure and arrival only—no custom messages.
  • Announce the stops you pass, even if you do not stop at every station.
  1. Walk to any station desk and begin a mission while your train remains out.
  2. Back the first passenger cart over the center of the yellow circle; its position differs by station.
  3. Sound the horn until passengers actively board the cart.
  4. Pick up and drop off at every station. Each mission passenger is worth $100.
  5. To park during a transportation mission, cancel the mission first.
  • Conductors may not be robbed, shot, or bothered while operating the train. Once you step off for repairs or other work, you are vulnerable.
  • You are not required to stop for people on the tracks, especially at full speed. Being on the tracks is unlawful; conductors are not responsible for resulting deaths.
  • Slow by at least 30 mph when entering Armadillo, Blackwater, Valentine, Saint Denis, Annesburg, and Rhodes.
  • Use horns and bells through towns and at major roads, tracks, and bridges to warn the public.

No speed is worth a collision. Keep your route visible and your warnings clear.

  • Switch tracks using the left and right key binds. Some areas respond poorly, so slow down and confirm your switch.
  • Use /on duty and /off duty. Before going off duty, confirm no player pickup request is waiting.
  • Announce each segment: Blackwater to Flatneck, Flatneck to Valentine, Valentine to Saint Denis, and onward.
  • When other trains are active, coordinate through county chat in the Western Skies Discord. Preventing a collision protects every passenger and both crews.
